## 📖 About The Project

**AI Smart Skill Coach** is an AI-powered intelligent learning platform that helps students and professionals learn effectively from their own documents.

Upload your PDFs, notes, or lecture materials — and our AI will understand them completely. Ask any question, and get accurate answers directly from YOUR content, not generic internet knowledge.

It's like having a **personal tutor** who has memorized all your study materials and guides you based on your unique learning needs.

## 🔥 Core Features

### 1️⃣ Smart Document Understanding (RAG Technology)

When you upload your PDFs or notes, our AI reads and truly understands them. When you ask questions, it searches through YOUR documents and provides accurate answers with source references.

**How it works:**
1. 📄 You upload your documents (PDFs, notes, lectures)
2. 🧠 AI divides them into smart, searchable chunks
3. ❓ You ask any question about the content
4. 🔍 AI finds the most relevant sections
5. ✅ You get accurate answers with page/section references

**Benefits:**
- ✅ 100% answers based on YOUR content
- ✅ Dramatically reduced wrong answers
- ✅ Source citations with every response
- ✅ No more AI making things up

---

### 2️⃣ Domain Expert AI (Fine-Tuned Models)

Regular AI knows a little about everything but isn't an expert in anything. We train our AI to become a **specialist** in specific domains — like having a subject matter expert at your fingertips.

**Available Domains:**

| Domain | What It Covers |
|--------|----------------|
| 💻 **IT & Programming** | Coding, software development, tech concepts |
| 🏥 **Medical** | Medical terminology, health concepts, case studies |
| 💼 **Business** | Management, finance, business strategies |
| 📚 **Academic** | General education, exam preparation |

**Benefits:**
- ✅ Domain-specific accurate answers
- ✅ Understands technical terminology correctly
- ✅ Provides field-specific examples and explanations
- ✅ Like talking to a subject expert

---

### 3️⃣ Personalized Learning Intelligence

Our AI **remembers you** — your learning history, your weak areas, and your progress. It acts as a personal tutor who guides you on what to study and where to improve.

**What AI Tracks:**
- 📊 Topics you've studied
- ⏱️ Time spent on each topic
- ❌ Mistakes in quizzes
- 📈 Your overall progress and trends

**Smart Recommendations:**
- *"This topic needs revision — you scored low here"*
- *"Focus on this concept — you're taking longer than usual"*
- *"Complete these 3 topics today for optimal progress"*
- *"Great job! You've improved 20% this week!"*

**Benefits:**
- ✅ Personalized study plans
- ✅ Automatic weak area detection
- ✅ Smart daily recommendations
- ✅ Visual progress tracking with insights

---

### 4️⃣ Paid Assessments & Verified Certificates

Test your knowledge with professional assessments and earn verified certificates that you can add to your resume or LinkedIn profile.

**Subscription Plans:**

| Plan | Price | What You Get |
|------|-------|--------------|
| 🆓 **Free** | ₹0 | 5 documents, 50 questions/day, basic quizzes |
| ⭐ **Pro** | ₹799/month | Unlimited documents & questions, all quizzes, 3 certificates/month |
| 👑 **Premium** | ₹1,499/month | Everything in Pro + priority support, custom domain training |

**Certificate Process:**
1. 📝 Take a paid assessment
2. 🎯 Score 80% or above
3. 🏆 Receive your verified certificate
4. 📥 Download PDF or share directly
5. ✅ Anyone can verify using unique certificate ID

**Benefits:**
- ✅ Industry-recognized certificates
- ✅ Add to resume & LinkedIn
- ✅ QR code verification system
- ✅ Multiple domain certifications available
