## 6. Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

### Primary USP:
> **"Learn from YOUR documents with AI that truly understands your content — no hallucinations, just accurate answers."**

### Supporting USPs:
1. **RAG-Powered Accuracy** — Answers only from your uploaded documents
2. **Domain Expert AI** — Fine-tuned for IT, Medical, Business
3. **Smart Personalization** — AI tracks your progress and weak areas
4. **Verified Credentials** — Certificates with verification system
