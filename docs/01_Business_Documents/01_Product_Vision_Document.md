## 1. Executive Summary

**AI Smart Skill Coach** ek revolutionary AI-powered learning platform hai jo traditional e-learning ki limitations ko solve karti hai. Ye platform RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) aur Fine-Tuned AI models ka use karke users ko unke own documents se personalized learning experience provide karti hai.

---

## 2. Vision Statement

> *"Har learner ke paas ek intelligent, personalized AI tutor ho jo unke study materials ko samjhe, weak areas identify kare, aur verified certifications provide kare — education ko accessible, personalized, aur result-oriented banaye."*

---

## 3. Problem Statement

### Current Learning Challenges:

| Problem | Impact |
|---------|--------|
| **Information Overload** | Students ke paas bahut documents hain but efficiently seekhna mushkil |
| **Generic AI Limitations** | ChatGPT/Bard general answers dete hain, specific documents se nahi |
| **AI Hallucinations** | AI wrong answers generate karta hai jo misleading hain |
| **No Personalization** | Learning platforms user ki individual needs nahi samajhte |
| **No Progress Tracking** | Weak areas identify nahi hote, improvement measure nahi hota |
| **Certification Gap** | Self-study ke baad koi valid certification nahi milti |
